01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Pawfect Match is a mobile app design concept that combines pet food shopping with accessible pet care information. The app allows users to explore food products for cats and dogs while learning about everyday pet care, nutrition, common health concerns, and essential pet care tips.

The project aims to create a friendly, organised, and approachable mobile interface that makes pet-related information and shopping easier to navigate.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

My design approach focused on creating a welcoming and easy-to-understand mobile interface. I developed the final screen designs directly in Figma, exploring a warm visual identity through neutral colours, pet photography, rounded interface elements, and consistent navigation.

I organised the screens around three main areas: browsing pet food products, accessing pet care information, and exploring brand-related content. My focus was on maintaining visual consistency and making the different sections feel connected.

  1. 01

    Friendly Visual Identity

    Warm neutral colours, pet imagery, and rounded interface elements create an approachable atmosphere.

  2. 02

    Organised Content

    Shopping, pet care guidance, and health information are separated into clear sections.

  3. 03

    Consistent Interface

    Repeated navigation patterns, icons, and layout styles help maintain consistency across the app.

03 / FINAL UI DESIGN

The final interface designs showcase the app's main screens, from welcoming users to exploring pet food products and accessing useful pet care information.

04 / DESIGN REFLECTION

Through Pawfect Match, I gained more experience in designing mobile interfaces with Figma, organising different types of content, and maintaining a consistent visual style across multiple screens.

The project helped me understand the importance of balancing visual appeal with clear navigation and readable information. It also encouraged me to think more carefully about how interface elements work together to create a cohesive mobile app experience.
